Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how Huali Tech Commercial Co., Ltd. handles information in connection with its internal Amazon Ads API integration and this website.

1. Who we are

Huali Tech Commercial Co., Ltd. operates the Splotiva brand and manages its own Amazon advertising accounts.

2. Information we process

When our internal team authorizes the Amazon Ads API integration, we may process information needed to manage and report on our own advertising accounts, including Amazon advertising profile identifiers, campaign and ad group information, keywords and targets, budgets and bids, advertising performance reports, and authorization credentials or tokens required for the integration.

This public website does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, payment information, or other sensitive personal information through a form.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to synchronize advertising data, prepare internal reports, monitor campaign performance, and support optimization of our own Amazon Ads campaigns.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell advertising data or provide our Amazon Ads API integration to third-party advertisers. We may use service providers that help us host or operate our internal systems, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and the security of our services.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, legal obligations, and legitimate business needs. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

6. Your choices and requests

For questions about this notice or requests concerning information handled by our internal advertising integration, contact us at hualigaoke@163.com.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
