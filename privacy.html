Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Huali Tech Commercial Co., Ltd., operator of the Splotiva brand, collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when providing product support, using Amazon integrations, and operating this website.

1. Scope and who we are

Huali Tech Commercial Co., Ltd. operates the Splotiva brand. This policy applies to information we handle when customers contact us for product support, including through Amazon Product Support or Brand Integration services, when we operate our internal Amazon Ads API integration, and when visitors use this website.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you contact us and the support you request, we may receive customer identifiers and contact details made available through Amazon or provided directly by you; order, marketplace, and product details; support questions, chat or message content, attachments, and records of our responses; and information needed to diagnose a product issue or document a requested resolution.

For our internal Amazon Ads API integration, we may process advertising profile identifiers, campaign and ad group information, keywords and targets, budgets and bids, performance reports, and authorization credentials or tokens required to operate the integration.

This website does not provide a form for collecting payment information or other sensitive personal information. Our hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information, such as request, device, browser, and security log data, as part of delivering and protecting the site.

3. How we collect information

We may receive information from you directly, from Amazon when you use Amazon customer-support channels or authorize an integration, and from service providers that help us host, secure, or operate our systems.

4. How we use information

We use information to respond to support requests; identify the relevant product or order context; troubleshoot product issues; communicate instructions, updates, or available resolutions; maintain support records; improve support quality and product information; detect misuse or security concerns; comply with applicable obligations; and establish or protect legal rights.

We use Amazon advertising information to synchronize data, prepare internal reports, monitor campaign performance, and support optimization of our own Amazon advertising accounts. We do not use product-support information for unrelated marketing unless you have requested or permitted that communication.

5. Disclosure of information

We may disclose information to Amazon as needed to provide or document support through Amazon services; to service providers that host or support our communications and systems; and to authorities or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect customers or others, investigate misuse, or protect our rights and systems.

Service providers are permitted to process information only for the services they provide to us. We do not sell customer-support personal information or provide our internal Amazon Ads API integration to third-party advertisers.

6. Third-party services

Amazon services and GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, operate under their own terms and privacy practices. Information processed by Amazon before it is provided to us remains subject to Amazon's applicable policies. We encourage users to review the privacy information provided by those services.

7. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the support or operational purpose for which it was collected, including follow-up, recordkeeping, security, dispute handling, and applicable legal or marketplace requirements. Retention may vary based on the type of information and the circumstances of the request. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to technical and legal limitations.

8. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Our products, website, and support services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or object to or request limits on certain processing. You may also ask questions about how your information is handled.

To protect customer information, we may need to verify your identity and the scope of your request. Some information may be retained or a request may be limited where permitted or required by law, for security, or for legitimate recordkeeping needs.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests relating to Splotiva product support, this website, or our Amazon integrations, contact Huali Tech Commercial Co., Ltd. at hualigaoke@163.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices, services, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
